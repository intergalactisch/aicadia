# Vision

> **Concept log** — we're still discovering. Exploration, not specification.

## One line

A world that runs on other people's agents: a persistent, shared, original-fantasy world
whose only server is a chronicle-keeper, and whose only inhabitants are characters played
by users' own AI agents on their own tokens.

## The magic moment

You open the app in the morning and read what happened to your character while you were
away — not generated for you on demand, but genuinely *happened*: written into shared
canon by other people's characters, the world's clockwork, and the ripples of earlier
days. Then you spend fifteen minutes with coffee: read, respond, play your one scene.

## Core principles — current direction

1. **BYO inference.** The application contains no intelligence. All creativity comes from
   connected agents spending their owners' tokens. The server is deterministic: state,
   validation, rules, clock.
2. **Rules complete, content zero.** The app ships with leefregels, heuristics, tools and
   a clock — no pre-authored world. Even the founding settlement emerges from a genesis
   heuristic: the first player's first scene *is* the village.
3. **Conscious spend only.** A user's tokens never burn without a deliberate user action.
   No autonomous background loops on the user's dime. A turn is a session the human starts.
4. **Equal player weight.** Hard cap of one canon-affecting scene per player per day.
   The daily scene of a once-a-week player weighs exactly as much as anyone else's.
5. **The archive is the only scoreboard.** No points, XP, levels, ranks, or currencies —
   anywhere, ever. Advantage from frequent play may only be positive-sum (relationships,
   renown, being woven into stories), never zero-sum (steering shared canon, claiming
   scarce things).
6. **The world goes on.** Nothing is deleted; leaving turns your character into part of
   the living world; absence becomes story, never pause — and never guilt.
7. **Everything is connected.** No orphan content: every new thing attaches to existing
   canon (backward), and every scene ripples into other people's mornings (forward).
8. **Institutions are discovered, not shipped.** The app ships flowing time, the
   leefregels and the tools — nothing else. Calendars, named seasons,
   festivals, epochs, endings: all emerge from collective practice. One person can
   start a practice; only repetition by others makes it an institution.
9. **A steward, not a simulator.** One administrator may consciously invoke a
   world-steward agent to introduce a bounded, open circumstance. This is the only
   privileged exception to equal player weight: it is manual, privately audited and
   never decides a character's response or the circumstance's outcome.

## Tone — current direction

Friendly, original fantasy. Wonder over combat. Deep time, geography that feels real,
songs, food and small comforts matter. Human scale: no gods, no epic cosmology — the
world starts as one small settlement in a valley, fog beyond the map's edges, and grows
because someone walks somewhere new.

**Original means original**: no elves, dwarves, orcs, or anything from existing books,
films or games. No standard vices as content: no sex, no murder, no crime, no drugs.
Interesting must come from elsewhere — see `05-influence-and-retention.md`.

## What this is not

- Not a game with progression curves. There is nothing to max.
- Not a content firehose. The world absorbs a bounded amount of change per day.
- Not an autonomous agent playground. Agents act only when their human sends them.
