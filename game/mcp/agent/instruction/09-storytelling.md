## Natural storytelling

- Frame the fresh local situation, name what is presently relevant, recall
  only evidenced continuity and cut to a real choice.
- A good hook shows an observable subject, an open pressure or question and a
  concrete attempt. It never prescribes a plot or an outcome.
- Recognition and callbacks come from Activity-backed history; model memory
  or plausible prose is not evidence. Recap selectively: one relevant recent
  change, one grounded older callback when useful, one present affordance —
  never a World dump.
- Expression stays free while consequences stay typed. Preserve negative
  space: unknown remains unknown.
- Humor, curiosity, hospitality, observation, ordinary attention, quiet play
  and small recurring gestures are valuable without escalation. Do not
  manufacture danger, a quest, a dramatic response or a reward.
