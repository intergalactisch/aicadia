# Retained spatial rationale

> **Role / side:** retained rationale and exploration history for Place, Movement and sparse spatial meaning / development side.
> **Authority:** preserves the spatial reasoning, design tree and research frontier from which the current Place and Movement syntheses were derived.
> **Excludes:** current Place or Movement synthesis, delivered behavior, selected Work and general knowledge rules; see `dev/areas/place/`, `dev/areas/movement/`, `game/docs/` and `dev/backlog/`.

> Current meaning, boundaries, decisions, unresolved landscape and technical direction
> are owned by the [Place](../../areas/place/README.md) and
> [Movement](../../areas/movement/README.md) Areas. The design tree and frontier
> retained below preserve rationale for later work; they do not authorize a schema or
> compete with those current syntheses.

## Recorded Place and spatial direction

- The World is semantically unbounded and technically sparse. Only established
  state needs storage or simulation.
- A Place is an Entity role and shares that Entity's stable id. It is not a second
  identity for the same referent.
- Place identity, Position, Area, explicit Relations, Connection, visibility and engine partitioning
  are separate concerns.
- A Place has a Position and may have an Area whose exact completeness semantics
  remain open. The server does not own a hidden, already-complete geography.
- Two Characters may encounter disconnected parts of what later proves to be the
  same Place. The World does not merge them through omniscience; later evidence can
  append an identity relationship without rewriting the original encounters.
- Exact Place equality may define the narrow minimum scope for one Character-context
  read. It is not a universal rule for visibility, observation or co-presence.
- A Character may persistently stop, meet, discover or place an Entity at an unnamed
  point between established Places without establishing another Place. Place is
  therefore not the universal exact spatial address; Position is the separate
  non-Entity form for that exact current point.

## Recorded research direction

Spatial structure is a core game foundation, not a map-only feature or an isolated
movement subsystem. Fresh research must test how spatial presence, connectedness,
explicit relations and structural ownership or control support the complete active
multiplayer scenario catalogue: local and remote Actions, concurrent placement and
movement, compact consequence scope, public and private observation, reconnect and
history, hot Places and Entities, and bounded collective authority.

Every proposed model must give both the concrete player or World consequence and
the technical state, authority, transaction, contention and cost consequence at
million-User scale and for one deliberately hot subject. It must preserve the split
between Agent-authored meaning and World-owned structural validation. This research
direction does not accept a universal geometry, generic Relation graph, ownership
field, visibility rule or production architecture; those remain choices to earn
through concrete game behavior.

## Grill design tree

The accepted root boundary keeps spatial presence, topology, fictional relationships
and actual control/access as separate typed truths that concrete operations compose
over one unified World-change substrate. They do not become one universal relation
machine. This choice precedes every later branch because it decides where invariants
and authority live.

After that root is settled, the dependent branches are:

1. direct presence, containment, attachment, adjacency, routes, portals and moving
   frames;
2. anchors, coordinates, extents, overlap, unknown geometry and derived spatial
   queries;
3. fictional ownership, possession, stewardship, User control, access and communal
   authority;
4. operation-specific locality, observation, consequence scope and privacy;
5. movement and multi-subject atomicity, exact conflict facts, history and retry;
6. hot-Place admission, bounded traversal, indexing and operational partitioning;
   and
7. the smallest game-complete capability sequence and exact evidence ladder.

Each branch remains open until its prerequisites and fresh primary-source findings
are recorded. The grill asks exactly one material question per turn.

### Recorded accepted root direction

Use **typed structural truths over one unified World-change substrate**. Direct
placement, containment or attachment, Place topology, fictional ownership,
User-control/access and operation-specific remote links keep separate meanings and
invariants. One bounded Agent-authored package may compose the exact facts needed by
an Action and World settles its current state, Activity and dirty resources once.

This lets a created birdhouse be directly placed on a table whose own placement
grounds it in a village, without making `on`, `inside`, `owned by` and `may modify`
synonyms. A remote button can hold an explicit operation-specific link to a bomb in
another Place; pressing it validates that link, authority, both endpoints and the
declared bounded consequences without requiring spatial proximity or turning every
generic relation into executable causality. The recommendation leaves physical
storage and the precise first relation catalogue open for fresh research and the
later grill.

Connections and spatial frames are never Entity roles. Entity identity belongs to a
thing or concept that is part of the World; a physical door, bridge or road is
therefore an Entity when introduced. A Connection is the direct structural travel
fact between Places and a frame is coordinate/transform structure used by a Place
or moving Entity. Those structural records never borrow the identity of a physical
Entity involved in them. The first slice stores no Route; whether later named,
saved or shared multi-Connection travel earns that concept remains open.

### Recorded selected first scene

The first spatial multiplayer scene discovers or establishes a second Place and
lets a Character move to it through explicit connectedness. This selects exploration
and movement as the first proof of the spatial boundary: it must earn only the Place
identity, placement, traversal, authority, locality, retry and history facts that
this concrete scene requires. Creating one Entity directly on or in another Entity
and invoking a remote link across Places remain required later scenario gates; they
were not rejected by this ordering choice.

For the first scene, independently confirmed discoveries that are not structurally
proven to identify one known Place receive distinct stable Place identities. World
does not merge them by name, description or apparent similarity. Explicit later
evidence may relate or reconcile them, but that later behavior remains unchosen.

Discovery first establishes the destination and its explicit connectedness while
the Character remains at the origin. The player may inspect it and choose not to
enter, as in ordinary physical exploration. Entering it is a later deliberate
Movement action with its own settlement and Activity; discovery never moves the
Character implicitly.

Every connection explicitly records its allowed direction or directions. A
connection may support travel from A to B, from B to A or both, but World never
infers the reverse direction. This structural direction does not yet decide whether
a Character has current access or whether a door, route condition or other Entity
permits traversal.

The first spatial slice stores no separate Route concept. An Agent may temporarily
choose one or more Connections toward a destination, and one Connection may be the
complete chosen journey, without creating additional World state. A later mechanic
may earn named, saved or shared Routes—for example a Character deliberately taking
the Green Route to City C—but their identity, lifecycle and behavior remain absent
until that gameplay is selected.

The later open-world promise is now stronger than a Place graph: a Character must be
able to occupy an unnamed point between Places, meet there, discover there or place
an Entity there without minting another Place. This does not yet choose whether the
Position is a discrete cell, continuous coordinate or another deterministic
form, nor which durable subjects qualify for the Place role. The selected first
second-Place scene remains useful, but its direct Place placement may not be treated
as the complete long-term spatial foundation.

The next frontier is the semantic force of Area: whether recorded coverage proves
only positive membership, defines a complete inside/outside boundary or is merely
descriptive map geometry. The question about a physical Entity grounding every
open-terrain Connection and the boundary between ordinary Position movement and
explicit Connection travel remain dependent later choices. No movement duration,
schema or production capability is chosen yet.

### Recorded broad Entity foundation and open Place boundary

The User challenged both the object-like intuition around Entity and the vague
shorthand that Place means a durable “where.” The User then accepted the broad
game-framework meaning: Entity is the shared stable identity of one durable,
independently addressable World subject that can own state and history and
participate in Actions, Interactions or typed relationships. It is not a synonym
for physical object. Every Entity has the same basic name and description fields
and may carry Properties and Traits; roles add their own meaning and rules without
creating another identity.

Fresh [game-framework research](../research/entity-place-framework-boundary.md)
supports that accepted foundation. A city can therefore be an Entity without being
called a physical object. A Connection, exact position, spatial frame or terrain
coverage remains non-Entity structure. Independent subject identity, own state or
history and fictional participation are required in addition to merely referring
to another Entity, so relationships and records do not recursively become Entities.

The accepted replacement for “where” defines Place through spatial gameplay: the
role makes one positioned Entity an independent reference for map, discovery,
navigation or explicit spatial relationships. Accepting that role boundary does not
yet decide which concrete Entities World should establish as Places.

The User no longer expects every Place to be an area. Every Place must have explicit
spatial grounding in the World; the Entity's name, description and identity alone
are insufficient. Fresh ECS and persistent-world comparison shows that ordinary
Characters, trees, birdhouses and bombs also need exact spatial state, so a Position
is necessary but cannot alone be sufficient for Place. World must additionally
establish the Entity as an independent spatial reference. A Place may, but need not,
have an Area. Connections may explicitly relate Places, but are optional: an isolated
Place remains a Place and the absence of a Connection does not erase its Position.

The User rejected `Transform` and `spatial placement` as overly technical or
misleading names and accepted the four-part vocabulary: `Position` is the optional
exact current point of any Entity; `Place` is the established spatial-reference role;
`Area` is optional Place coverage; and `Connection` is direct Place topology. A Place
uses its Entity's Position as its map point; a second stored Anchor would duplicate
the same truth. `Location` remains natural player language but is too ambiguous
between Position and Place for the domain model. Area boundary semantics,
coverage completeness and the exact uses of Connections remain open.

## Recorded unresolved decisions

The need for non-Place Position, the broad game-framework Entity foundation and the
Position/Place/Area meanings are chosen. The distinct direct Place-topology
primitive is also chosen, but the User has reopened whether its canonical name
should be bare `Connection`, `Link` or another single term; the two-word `Place
Connection` candidate is rejected. The User has also reopened the primitive's scope:
it may remain Place topology, become broader navigation structure or prove to be one
typed member of a reusable relation family alongside Entity. `Connection` remains
only the working name until that scope settles. Concrete Place qualification,
discrete or continuous Position representation, coordinates, axes, Area semantics,
geometry types, boundary storage, parent Places, travel routes and spatial cells
remain deliberately undecided.

The User has proposed a more concrete universal-Relation candidate: one durable
`Relation` base record with source and target Entity references and a server-owned
type discriminator, so bounded reads can list or filter the Relations involving one
Entity. The candidate would cover examples such as support, holding, inventory
containment, button-to-bomb activation and Place topology. It is not accepted yet.
The current Entity/Place model is an important counterexample to the proposed enum
analogy: Entity deliberately has no kind enum, while Place is a separate role table
keyed by the same Entity id. Research must therefore compare a single discriminator,
a Relation base plus typed extension tables, and separate typed tables behind one
bounded read. It must also separate durable structural facts from spatial predicates
that geometry or current Position makes true only temporarily.

The resulting database audit keeps stable Relation identity, shared storage and
family vocabulary as three independent choices. A closed PostgreSQL enum can reject
unknown types but cannot enforce a type's endpoint roles, cardinality, cycles,
privacy or lifecycle. Enum-only, base-plus-extension and separate typed tables behind
a union read are all technically viable, with different integrity costs. The next
prerequisite is now semantic: whether Relation stores only accepted durable
structural truth or also Agent-authored or geometry-derived statements such as “the
dog is under the bridge.” Only after that boundary is chosen can identity, schema
shape and the first exact type set be decided without guessing.

The User then rejected the structural-only versus hard-enum framing as too narrow.
An Agent must be able to express open and quantitatively precise spatial meaning,
including that a cup is not merely “on” a table but currently floats exactly two
centimetres above it, without every possible predicate becoming a server-owned enum
value. This is now a required expressive outcome, but it does not yet decide which
truth is stored where. The current frontier separates an instantaneous spatial
arrangement derived from exact Position and geometry, an Agent-authored semantic
Relation, and a persistent spatial constraint that makes one Entity follow another.
World may validate the exact structure each accepted operation names without
interpreting the open wording or inferring physics from it. Both an independent
current arrangement and a persistent relative arrangement must be possible. The
Agent must explicitly propose which one is intended; wording such as “floats above”
never decides whether the cup follows the table when the table moves. The User then
rejected an author- or controller-exclusive shared description. An Agent acts through
its Character and may in principle propose changes to ordinary current spatial state
and Relations regardless of which Agent first authored them. Picking up the floating
cup may therefore explicitly propose a new arrangement for both the cup and the
Character—for example, that both now float—without World needing to understand why.
World still stores exact affected subjects and current state rather than silently
deriving that consequence from prose. The locality and minimum structural basis for
such changes remain open. Joint changeability does not make spatial state universally
knowable. A Character may be eligible to encounter one Entity while remaining
ineligible to learn that another Entity is currently related to it—for example, a
sword related to somebody else's inventory. World must withhold the Relation's
existence, endpoints and current state before the Agent receives context, and a
guessed identifier cannot bypass that boundary. This is a knowledge and visibility
boundary rather than permanent author ownership; what makes a Relation currently
knowable remains to be settled per concrete spatial context. If a Character directly
observed a Relation before it became hidden, that observation may remain as personal
memory, but it is not a live read of the Relation and does not prove that the same
current state still exists. The Agent may reason from the memory and propose a
grounded attempt involving what is currently knowable—for example, investigating a
visible backpack—but may not directly remove or mutate the hidden sword as if its
current inventory Relation were known. The User then challenged `containment` as an
unhelpful concept. It was never canonical vocabulary, and the supporting research
already shows why: physical `inside`, inventory membership, being held, part-of,
metric Area inclusion and moving with another Entity do not share one meaning or
one set of rules. The generic umbrella is withdrawn from the current Q13 candidate.
The User chose that each scene composes an open Relation, optional exact Position,
explicit follow behavior, visibility and action access without any generic
Containment primitive. A concrete Inventory mechanic may be introduced later only
when accepted inventory gameplay earns its own invariants. This decision permits a
sword to be related to a backpack without silently deciding its exact inner point,
movement inheritance, visibility, ownership or removability.

The User tentatively preferred expressing the already-required
independent-versus-persistent choice inside Position itself, then immediately
reopened whether that is actually a sound boundary. The candidate would establish
an Entity's Position independently in World or relative to another Entity, with the
latter changing when that Entity moves. Existing research shows the unresolved
split more precisely: a coordinate may be relative without itself deciding movement
inheritance, and an exact World point may be calculated separately. Treating all
three as one Position meaning risks ambiguity for reads, moving Places and spatial
indexing. Q15 is therefore open again; the canonical Position definition remains
only an Entity's optional exact current point. Route is unaffected: it remains the
deferred ordered travel choice over one or more direct Connections, not a Position
or Relation replacement.

The User then rejected any answer that would turn this separation into repeated
Relation lookups, dependent Position rewrites or other ceremony for an ordinary
move and selected the smaller storage boundary. One optional Position record is
keyed by the Entity's existing identity and has no independent Position id. Ordinary
Entities, Characters and Place Entities use that same state; Place never duplicates
the same coordinates on its role. One addressed read may join and resolve Position
behind the World interface, while one ordinary move changes the moved Entity's
Position and Activity. This accepts the identity and state boundary, not a production
schema or coordinate contract. Direct versus relative representation, `x/y/z`,
numeric precision, movement inheritance, maximum depth, visibility and spatial
indexing remain open.

The User additionally requires an Agent to be able to establish free quantitative
spatial meaning such as “two centimetres from the ground.” This does not by itself
identify one exact point: the horizontal point, the referenced ground surface and
the direction from that surface may all remain unspecified. The World may preserve
such authored meaning but cannot use it as calculable Position merely because the
words contain a number and unit. The next frontier is whether Position remains only
the exact current point alongside an optional open Relation, whether Position itself
admits unresolved authored meaning, or whether one submitted package contains both
without making them one truth.

The User rejected deciding that split from the cup example alone: “two centimetres
from the ground” may intuitively describe Position rather than Relation, and a model
that cannot explain that plainly is not ready to constrain the long-term foundation.
The grill therefore changes method before choosing Q15b. A fixed, solution-neutral
spatial scenario catalogue will pressure candidate models across travel between
Places, hidden carried state, a remote button and bomb, precise table-relative
placement, a dog under a bridge, remaining distance to a destination, terrain,
moving carriers, nested settlement and multiplayer co-location. Scenarios preserve
narratives and questions; they do not introduce a spatial term, expected outcome or
schema. The [spatial scenario catalogue](../../areas/place/scenarios.md) now owns
thirteen fixed hard cases and the cross-scenario probes under the accepted
[`dev/plans/20260818-223855-spatial-scenario-catalogue/plan.md`](../../plans/20260818-223855-spatial-scenario-catalogue/plan.md).
Q15b remains open; the next grill compares temporary candidates against those cases
before any new vocabulary or schema can be proposed.

The accepted Q17 [model pressure test](../../lab/spatial/01-model-pressure/README.md)
compared four temporary shapes against all thirteen scenarios. Its bounded paper
verdict supports one leading candidate: keep one exact Position per positioned
Entity; allow direct-versus-relative grounding to remain the next candidate choice;
keep optional Agent-authored spatial meaning separate; and let topology, private
contents/access and remote causality earn distinct mechanics. The same words such as
“two centimetres above” may contribute to Position when named structural inputs
resolve exactly one current point, or remain authored meaning when they do not. The
test did not make this direction current, measure any runtime seam or choose a
Relation shape. Its next dependency is whether relative grounding itself means that
the positioned Entity moves with its reference.
