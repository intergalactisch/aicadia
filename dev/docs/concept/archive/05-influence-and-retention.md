# Influence and retention

> **Concept log** — we're still discovering. Exploration, not specification.

The central question: what can players influence — without vice-based stakes (no sex,
murder, crime, drugs) — such that they voluntarily want to return? Debate outcome:
separate **pull** (brings you back tomorrow) from **depth** (keeps you for a year); the
ranking inverts between them.

## Influence taxonomy — from the debate, adjusted

| # | Influence | Pull | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An owed response — someone addressed you | Highest | Low |
| 2 | A pending resolution you set in motion lands today | Highest | Low |
| 3 | Place-making — strangers route through what you built | Medium | Highest |
| 4 | Naming — a hill, a wind, a dish, a knot | Medium | High |
| 5 | Cultural authorship — your recipe becomes the festival dish | Low | Highest |
| 6 | Stewardship of rules and customs | Low | High |
| 7 | Care / tending (decay on state, never on archive) | Medium | Medium |
| 8 | Mystery / discovery (needs a supply mechanism) | High | Medium |
| 9 | Reputation / being cited | Medium | Low + hazardous |
| — | ~~Craft mastery by accumulation~~ | — | Deleted (XP in disguise) |

Notes:

- **Cultural authorship is the depth floor, not the daily hook** — its payoff lags by
  weeks. The daily hook is 1+2 (social reciprocity and pending resolutions); the yearly
  glue is 3+5. Don't ask either to do the other's job.
- **Place-making** is stronger than being cited: use beats mention ("someone I'll never
  meet used my bridge" — Death Stranding's proven positive-sum loop).
- **Attention is the only scarce resource** in a world without scarcity. Being read is
  the real reward; the morning report is the editorial layer that allocates it.

## Reputation guardrails — idea from the debate

Citation-counting Goodharts into clique formation (SCP wiki, academic citation rings).
Rules:

- Surface citations as **content, never totals**: "three people mentioned your bread
  this week — here's what they said." Named people, recent window, quoted text.
- Never a cumulative number, never a rank, never a leaderboard. (Displayed renown is
  points; undisplayed renown doesn't motivate; quoted renown is social.)

## Vulnerability — current thinking after the debate

The debate proposed a Belonging-Outside-Belonging token economy (weak moves earn tokens,
tokens buy strong scenes) to break *niceness deadlock* — the observed failure mode of
pastoral fiction where everyone is agreeable and nothing happens. **Rejected**: a
spendable token is a currency, and the no-points principle admits no exceptions.

Adopted instead, as world physics: **bad news travels faster.** The dawn engine weights
scenes of need, loss and failure to ripple further and reach more morning reports.
Trouble buys attention and incoming care — not spendable power. Watch for misery-farming
(performative suffering for reach); leefregel 11 and finite attention are the brakes.

## Safe tension sources, ranked — from the debate

1. **Mortality** — banned murder ≠ banned dying (see doc 04; OPEN).
2. **Incompatible goods** — the mill or the meadow; two good things that can't both
   happen. The engine of all village drama; needs no villain; infinite supply.
3. **Being misremembered** — a sourced false report remains in history beside the
   claim selected by a current projection.
4. **Scarcity of presence** — one place at a time; two things you love, same evening.
5. **Impersonal adversity** — weather, frost, the year without a summer. The only
   antagonist that can't be blamed, forgiven or negotiated with.
6. **Promises that become hard to keep** — public commitment + changed circumstance.
7. **Secrets and reticence** — the thing someone won't say; withholding harms no one.
8. **Shame and repair** — the botched bread at the festival; the forgotten name.
9. **Change itself** — the road arrives, the young leave; nostalgia as antagonist.
10. **Rivalry over distinction** — never over rank.

## Mystery supply — idea from the debate

Fog is a one-time resource, and if the first visitor simply invents what's there, nobody
can ever be *wrong* — and mystery without the possibility of being wrong is improv, not
mystery. Mechanism: **player-sealed envelopes.** A player leaving a place may seal a
secret about it, revealed only when someone else meets a stated condition. Zero server
intelligence, genuine discovery, and departure becomes generative.

## Anti-patterns — never build

- Cumulative scores, ranks, levels, leaderboards — in any costume.
- Spendable token currencies (including "vulnerability tokens").
- Guilt mechanics: visible hesitation labels, decaying pets, streak shaming.
- Social debt without expiry (the play-by-post killer).
- FOMO countdowns; anything that punishes absence instead of forgiving it.
- Deletion of player-made things as a neglect consequence (decay may touch state,
  never the archive).
- Per-player claims on shared direction (canon steering must never be purchasable
  by volume, tokens, or seniority).
- Mechanical authority of one player over another. Service, apprenticeship and
  leadership may exist as fiction; the server knows no masters — nobody can ever be
  *made* to do anything. Hierarchy as story, never as mechanics.
