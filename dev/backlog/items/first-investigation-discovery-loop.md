# First complete investigation and discovery loop

> **Role / side:** forward-planning item / development side.
> **Authority:** records this outcome's backlog state, dependencies and completion pointers.
> **Excludes:** current product contracts, decision rationale and detailed delivery evidence; see `game/docs/`, `dev/docs/concept/` and `dev/docs/evidence/`.

## Outcome

An entered Character's Agent grounds one investigation at the exact current Place.
World admits it, resolves uncertainty before the Agent authors new content and
answers zero or positive. A zero honestly ends one attempt without Activity; a
positive result may be completed—after the User confirms the authored find—as
exactly one found Entity with durable attempt/result provenance and Activity that a
co-present Character can observe. What already existed in the World is found this
way; what a Character makes or brings remains ordinary Action introduction.

The [current capability catalog](../../../game/docs/README.md#capability-catalog)
owns the accepted game contract, the [discovery
record](../../../dev/docs/concept/discovery.md#selected-complete-loop) owns rationale and
the [completed build
plan](../../plans/20260814-204007-first-investigation-discovery-loop/plan.md) retains
the execution boundary.

## Why it mattered

World entry, exact-Place orientation, Actions, Interactions, Properties and Traits
were delivered. The missing defining game outcome was World-authoritative discovery:
uncertainty resolved before Agent authorship and one accepted result shared through
current state and history. The former separate roll and commit rows would each stop
short of that player/World outcome, so the User selected and completed one whole
loop instead of shipping either piece as standalone plumbing.

## Completion boundary

The completed slice is the exact investigation/discovery outcome above, including
World-first uncertainty, explicit confirmation of the authored find, durable
attributable history and a co-present readback. The [start
contract](../../../game/docs/capability/start_investigation.md), [submit
contract](../../../game/docs/capability/submit_discovery.md) and [Agent
flow](../../../game/docs/agent.md#required-investigation-and-discovery-flow) own its
current mechanics.

## Open choices

None for this completed slice. Movement and discovery of further Places remain
separate queued outcomes.

## Dependencies

- exact-Place Entity, Activity and current Property/Trait reads;
- Character-grounded Action/Interaction and atomic Activity history;
- the committed uniform Entity-state baseline.

## Non-goals

Movement, further Places, adjacency/containment, geometry, several results per
attempt, rich World subject models, semantic inference, server-authored prose,
production rate-limiting infrastructure and paid-model validation.
