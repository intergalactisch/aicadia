# Spatial lab

> **Role / side:** retained spatial experiment track / development side.
> **Authority:** identifies each spatial lab artifact, its question and where to read it.
> **Excludes:** each experiment's verdict, accepted spatial game behavior, canonical vocabulary, production schema and sourced research conclusions; those remain in experiment front matter, `game/docs/`, `dev/CONTEXT.md`, accepted plans and `dev/docs/research/`.

This track pressure-tests candidate spatial models against fixed player and World
scenarios before any candidate can influence current terminology or implementation.
Its artifacts are non-authoritative and may deliberately refute apparently simple
models.

## Experiments

- [01 — Model pressure](01-model-pressure/README.md) — Which of four temporary model shapes best explains all thirteen fixed spatial scenarios without conflating exact Position, authored meaning, movement, topology, visibility or remote causality?

## Spatial-specific boundaries

- The fixed fixture lives in the Place Area's spatial scenario catalogue.
- Candidate labels, fields and tables remain lab-local.
- Paper reasoning proves no PostgreSQL, World, MCP, Agent or production-scale property.
- A surviving runtime uncertainty earns a later bounded technical experiment; it is never filled in by architectural confidence.
