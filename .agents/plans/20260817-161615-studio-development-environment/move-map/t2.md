model folders under `docs/game/model/`, plus the headings T2 renamed, removed or
added. `docs/game/domain.md` keeps its path and its `# Domain contract` title; every
listed source has exactly one destination. Anchor ids follow the repository rule (GitHub-compatible):
lower-case, punctuation removed, spaces to `-`, duplicates suffixed `-1`, `-2`.
