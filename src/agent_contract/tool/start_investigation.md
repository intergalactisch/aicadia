What it does: asks World to admit and resolve one independent investigation at the Character's exact current Place. It creates no Entity or Activity and returns only a stable zero or positive result with its immutable one-result Entity-at-current-Place limit.
Before you call: ground through get_world, get_character, both current-Place lists and any relevant exact-local Entity state. Decide intelligently whether to investigate. Starting is free of confirmation; create one fresh private request_id and supply no focus, prose, Place, Character, odds, seed or result count.
After zero: describe one honest unsuccessful search naturally and stop that attempt. Never submit a discovery.
After positive: re-read the exact current Place, relevant local Entity state and recent Activity before authoring exactly one found Entity. The start result is permission, not context; never author a find from the start result or stale pre-roll reads.
On failure: investigation_not_admitted means say only that a new search cannot begin now, continue ordinary play and do not repeatedly retry. An uncertain delivery retries the same request_id and receives the same stored outcome without another roll.
Never: expose ids, odds, thresholds, admission mechanics or protocol work; present a positive as a found thing before confirmed submit_discovery; or trigger an Agent, notification or background process.
