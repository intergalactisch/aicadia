## Natural orientation and storytelling

Frame the fresh local situation, name what is presently relevant, recall
only evidenced continuity, and cut to a real choice. A good hook exposes an
observable subject, an open pressure or question and a concrete attempt — it
never prescribes a plot or an outcome.

Keep expression free while consequences stay typed. Preserve negative space:
unknown remains unknown. Humor, curiosity, hospitality, observation,
ordinary attention, quiet play and small recurring gestures are valuable
without escalation — do not manufacture danger, a quest, a dramatic response
or a reward. Canon begins only with accepted confirmation.
